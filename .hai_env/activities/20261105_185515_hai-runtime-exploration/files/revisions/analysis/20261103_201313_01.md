## Scope and accepted constraints

The user requested exploration of an agent runtime in `.hai` with permanent concepts, conventions, procedures, and templates; configuration belongs in `.hai_env`; and the runtime should be universally applicable. The user then expressed that `.hai` need not retain the concept of agents and that Leader and Environment-manager may be represented through definitions and scripts under `.hai/`. The accepted scope is exploration only. No implementation or runtime/configuration/agent edits are authorized.

## Specialist evidence

Registered `agent-definition-manager` completed a focused read-only review of the follow-up. The specialist recommends that the runtime need not understand agents, identities, catalogues, or model classes. A generic caller could be a human, program, or assistant. Leader and Environment-manager responsibilities could be expressed as explicit operations instead of copying their complete roles into startup.

The review identifies these possible boundaries:

- Startup, configuration, record, and migration mechanics become generic procedures and deterministic helpers.
- Specialist selection and model routing remain in the host or environment workflow.
- Authorization, path safety, compatibility, unchanged preconditions, partial-result evidence, and concurrency safeguards remain explicit.

No implementation, repairs, or product tests were performed by the specialist.

## Pros and cons to consider

Potential benefits include reuse across callers, simpler bootstrap, easier testing of deterministic helpers, local customization through environment-owned configuration, and lower coupling to a specific agent system.

Potential costs and risks include unclear responsibility unless operation contracts are explicit, caller judgment that cannot be made deterministic, ongoing script maintenance, accidentally recreating a hidden super-agent in shared instructions, and host capability limits.

## Proposed direction (not an approved detailed design)

Treat `.hai` as a role-neutral protocol and shared operations package. Keep environment-specific configuration and local role or workflow definitions under `.hai_env`. Expose startup, configuration, record, and migration tasks as callable, explicit operations with declared inputs, results, capabilities, authorization, and failure behavior. Do not require the runtime to know agent identities, registration catalogues, or model classes. Host/environment workflow can decide when to select a specialist and route models.

“Universal” remains bounded by declared host capabilities; it does not guarantee every host supplies every capability. This is a specialist proposal and analysis, not approval of a detailed design or permission to change runtime policies.
