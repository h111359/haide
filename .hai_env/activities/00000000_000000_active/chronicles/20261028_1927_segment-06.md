I recommend **automatic recording in batches, with a mandatory recording check before closing an Activity**.

The current rules allow deferred recording but do not give it a definite deadline. That is what needs to change.

**For ordinary conversation:**

1. **Activity-manager requests periodic recording** when an Activity starts or resumes and chronicles are enabled. This request remains applicable; you do not need to repeat it.

2. **Leader checks whether recording is due at the beginning of each turn.** My proposed defaults are:
   - **5 completed exchanges** waiting to be recorded; or
   - **10 minutes** since the oldest newly pending exchange.

   Whichever happens first triggers a batch. This is a small check, without invoking another agent just to check the threshold.

3. **Chronicles-manager records the batch alongside other work where the runtime supports it.** Leader does not normally wait for recording before answering. Only one recording batch runs at a time.

4. **Progress is saved with the Activity.** It identifies what was verified, what remains pending, and any unresolved gaps. A restart or a new chat therefore does not lose the backlog. Failed recording remains pending, with spaced retries rather than repeated attempts on every operation.

The ten-minute condition is checked when the conversation is active; it does not imply a timer running after the application stops. Where background execution is unavailable, occasional recording may add some delay, but batching avoids paying that cost on every operation.

**Before Activity closure, recording becomes mandatory:**

1. Activity-manager requests a fresh recording and verification through Leader.
2. Chronicles-manager records and verifies all required exchanges through the latest saved conversation, including the closure request.
3. Activity-manager closes the Activity only after receiving confirmation that no required exchanges remain unrecorded or unverified.

If recording fails or source text is unavailable, **the Activity stays open**, with the reason reported. Under this rule, the three previously identified unverified responses would currently prevent closure.

5. **Before closure, Activity-manager requires fresh confirmation that recording is complete.** This remains its responsibility because it controls closure.

So Activity-manager is involved in **Activity creation/discovery and closure**, rather than every recording batch.

There is one technical limit: **a `SKILL.md` instruction cannot itself create a persistent background service.** The tools available here support parallel agents, but do not establish that a worker can keep waking periodically after Leader’s response ends. Official OpenAI documentation describes local scheduled tasks, which require the computer and application to remain running. [Scheduled tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app)

For reliable periodic execution, the host needs a scheduler or a small background helper that wakes Chronicles-manager when needed. That also avoids keeping an AI agent continuously polling.

**I recommend adopting your ownership model**, with persistent background recording where supported and automatic catch-up during active turns elsewhere. Neither mode should require repeated user requests. No changes made yet.
