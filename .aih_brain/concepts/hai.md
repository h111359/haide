# HAI

## Definition

[HAI] is a framework for AI assisted development.

## Purpose

[HAI] defines a routine for using AI in agentic mode to build or modify a digital product in a [workspace].
[HAI] consist of [brain] and [memory]

## Supporting Terms

- [workspace]: one or several folders and their subfolder structures where the product is located
