# Glossary convention

## Type

`glossary`

Definition and classification scope: [Glossary](../references/specification.md#glossary).

## Content file

`content.md`, in Markdown, alongside the leaf's `__ncf__.md`.

## Required content

- **Vocabulary scope:** the domain or subject covered by this glossary.
- **Entries:** canonical term, precise meaning, applicable context, and a stable heading for linking.
- **Alternative names:** acronyms, synonyms, translations, and deprecated or misleading names where relevant.
- **Distinctions and examples:** related terms, units, illustrative uses, and common misunderstandings.
- **Sources and unknowns:** authoritative references and unresolved terminology conflicts.

## Writing rules

- Define each term in one canonical glossary entry and link to it from other leaves.
- Make context-dependent meanings explicit; avoid circular definitions.
- Link rules, entity structures, and field definitions instead of embedding them in term descriptions.
