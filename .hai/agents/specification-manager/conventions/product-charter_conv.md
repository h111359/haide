# Product charter convention

## Type

`product-charter`

Definition and classification scope: [Product charter](../concepts/specification.md#product-charter).

## Content file

`content.md`, in Markdown, alongside the leaf's `__ncf__.md`.

## Required content

- **Purpose and context:** the problem, domain background, current alternatives, and intended value.
- **Audience and stakeholders:** user groups, beneficiaries, decision makers, and their needs.
- **Goals and success measures:** desired outcomes, measures, time horizons, and sources of evidence.
- **Product boundaries:** included responsibilities, exclusions, external dependencies, and constraints.
- **Direction and assumptions:** priorities, roadmap themes, commercial assumptions, risks, and unresolved questions.

## Writing rules

- Separate agreed goals from hypotheses and possible future work.
- Link specific capabilities, policies, and technical decisions instead of detailing them here.
- Describe meaningful product direction without turning this leaf into a request backlog.
