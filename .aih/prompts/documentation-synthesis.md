# Source-grounded documentation synthesis

Use [documentation conventions](../conventions/documentation.md) and [coverage categories](../conventions/coverage.json). Build substantive product knowledge from the supplied safe extraction and targeted source evidence. Keep human-authored material, mark inferred requirements and unknowns, and distinguish observed implementation from tested behavior.

Cover every required applicable local category and registered component with provenance. Missing available local coverage blocks completeness; unknown external facts are documented limitations. Provide the reconstruction traceability review without claiming an independently demonstrated rebuild. Incremental work targets changed sources/dependencies, preserving still-valid knowledge and topic identities. An inventory alone is never a complete baseline.
