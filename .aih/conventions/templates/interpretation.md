# Canonical request interpretation

Record request ID, interpretation revision, source submissions and workspace revision.

## Problem and intended outcome

Preserve attributed user meaning and explain why the change is needed.

## Current and required behavior

Separate observed current behavior, accepted required behavior and proposed inferences.

## Scope and constraints

List affected root-qualified areas, exclusions, stakeholders and explicit limitations.

## Acceptance criteria

Assign stable requirement IDs and observable expected results, with evidence/test links.

## Assumptions and consequential questions

Record remaining uncertainties, ownership, question revision and impact on readiness.

## Source and amendment disposition

Link submitted answers/amendments, superseded directions and unresolved contradictions.
