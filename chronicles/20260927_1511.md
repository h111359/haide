```

- `{S1:L40-45}` identifies supporting evidence.
- `Details: {D3:section "Field constraints"}` identifies the detailed specification belonging to the finding.

Keeping `Details:` makes that relationship explicit. A document’s existence does not itself establish evidence or acceptance.

# Referenced documents

- **D3:** [Customer field definitions](extensions/data-dictionary/customer-fields.csv)
  Type: data-dictionary
- **D8:** [Customer API](extensions/interface-contract/customer-api.yaml)
  Type: interface-contract
```

```markdown
Details: {D3:field "customer_id"}

```

Prefer named fields, operations, and sections over line numbers for maintained extensions, because edits frequently shift lines. Whole-document references such as `Details: {D3}` can be allowed when the entire document applies.

One important rule: **using a document as a source and using it as a detailed specification are different relationships**. If a document serves both purposes, preserve both references rather than allowing `Details:` to replace the evidence citation.
